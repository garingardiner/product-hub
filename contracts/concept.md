# Contract: Concept (phase 1, Discovery)

**Purpose:** Decide whether an idea is worth validating with customers.
**Consumers:** PM leadership, User research, UX, Sales.
**Default form:** `claude-doc` (narrative, commentable). One page is the target.

## Must contain
- Problem statement: who has the problem, how often, what it costs them
- Initial user requirements (seed `requirements.yaml` with draft IDs)
- Competitive landscape: how competitors handle it, where we can differentiate (use `competitive-brief`)
- Hypotheses to validate in the Feedback phase, each phrased so it can be proven wrong
- Rough sizing guess: which segments, how many customers (to be validated)
- Recommendation: proceed / park / kill, and why

## Next phase takes
The hypotheses and target segments, which define who to interview and what to ask.

## Approved when
A PM lead and at least one collaborator (research or UX) have reviewed it and the recommendation is "proceed".
